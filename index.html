Tipper Track Privacy Policy

Effective date: 21 September 2026

Manrup Pty Ltd ("we", "us", or "our") operates the Tipper Track mobile application ("Tipper Track" or the "App").

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, how it may be stored or shared, and the choices available to you when using Tipper Track.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use Tipper Track, we may collect information including:

  • Account information: your email address and account/user identifier.
  • Business information: information you enter about your transport business, including trucks, trailers, drivers, customers, plant hire, jobs and related business records.
  • Financial and business records: income, expenses, driver payments, registration costs, finance or instalment information, and other financial information that you choose to enter into the App.
  • User-generated content: information and records that you enter into the App for the purpose of managing your transport business.
  • Usage and diagnostic information: limited technical, usage or diagnostic information may be processed when necessary for the operation, maintenance and reliability of the App.

2. Information We Do Not Intentionally Collect

Tipper Track does not intentionally request or collect precise location information, contacts, health information, photos, videos, audio recordings, browsing history, or advertising information as part of the normal operation of the App.

3. How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • provide and operate Tipper Track;
  • authenticate your account;
  • store and display your business records;
  • calculate and display business income, expenses and reports;
  • provide truck, driver, job and business management features;
  • maintain, troubleshoot and improve the App;
  • protect the security and integrity of the App; and
  • comply with applicable legal obligations.

4. Cloud Storage and Service Providers

Information submitted to Tipper Track may be stored and processed using third-party cloud and technology service providers that help us operate the App.

These providers may process information on our behalf for purposes such as authentication, database storage, application infrastructure and technical operation.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information.

We may disclose information to service providers where reasonably necessary to operate Tipper Track, or where disclosure is required or permitted by law.

We do not use your business information for third-party advertising.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information from unauthorised access, loss, misuse, alteration or disclosure. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Data Retention

We generally retain account and business information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide Tipper Track and maintain your business records, or as otherwise required or permitted by law.

8. Your Choices and Privacy Requests

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information we hold about you.

If you have a privacy question or would like to make a privacy request, please contact Manrup Pty Ltd using the support contact provided on the Tipper Track App Store listing.

9. Children's Privacy

Tipper Track is a business management application and is not intended for use by children.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the App, our practices, or applicable legal requirements. The updated policy will be published at this location with a revised effective date.
